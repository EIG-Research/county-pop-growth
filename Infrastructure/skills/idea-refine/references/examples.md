# Idea-Refine Examples

Use these abbreviated examples to calibrate mode, option granularity, and handoff
boundaries. Do not copy their substantive claims into a live session.

## Research Question

**Input:** "Refine an idea about declining labor-force participation among prime-age
workers."

**Fixed inputs:** A public-facing EIG research project for a policy audience; national
prime-age participation is the topic. Causal intent and data design remain open.

**Material directions:**

1. Build a descriptive geography-and-demography decomposition. The central bet is that
   composition and place reveal more than the aggregate trend. It fails if harmonized
   measures cannot separate margins consistently. The evidence gap is source and
   sample feasibility; the first check is a vintage-specific source and cell-size audit.
2. Study a policy discontinuity or staggered exposure. The central bet is that a
   defensible source of quasi-experimental variation exists. It fails if exposure is
   endogenous or timing anticipatory. The evidence gap is the actual institutional and
   identification record; the first check is a treatment-timing and pre-trend audit.
3. Validate a measurement-first series comparing survey definitions. The central bet
   is that apparent decline partly reflects measurement or denominator choices. It
   fails if series move together after harmonization. The evidence gap is cross-source
   comparability; the first check is a small harmonized-series comparison.

**Correct convergence:** Do not label direction two superior merely because it is
causal. Inspect identification and data evidence first. If these are unresolved,
recommend the cheapest diagnostic that separates a viable causal design from a
descriptive or measurement contribution.

## Policy or Communications Product

**Input:** "We should publish something showing where housing costs are squeezing
workers."

**Material directions:**

- a one-time empirical brief explaining a newly measured pattern;
- a recurring internal monitor for identifying places worth deeper reporting; or
- a public explanatory tool optimized for local lookup.

These directions differ because the audience use, evidentiary burden, production
cadence, and maintenance commitment differ. A chart redesign alone is not a direction.
The concept brief must exclude attractive adjacent work such as forecasting, causal
claims, or a recurring public product when those are not part of the selected use.

## Analytical Tool or Code Feature

**Input:** "Add a smarter way to keep generated adapters in sync."

**Repository-grounded directions:**

- extend the existing canonical validation to catch one additional drift class;
- add a human-readable diagnostic that explains an existing failed check; or
- redesign generation ownership around a new manifest.

The first two are narrow and reversible. The third may create a migration and a new
source of truth. Inspect the current generator and validation scripts before calling
any direction feasible or differentiated. Do not turn the comparison into an
implementation plan.

## Workflow or Process

**Input:** "Our review process catches issues too late. Refine a better approach."

**Material directions:**

- move one high-value check earlier in the pipeline;
- add a bounded pre-analysis design review for high-risk projects; or
- improve the final review's evidence trace so correction is faster.

Each direction targets a different bottleneck: prevention, early decision quality, or
recovery cost. The smallest useful pilot should measure a real outcome such as late
rework or unresolved findings, not checklist completion. At concept stage, recommend a
bounded asynchronous preflight or another direction without fixing the exact pilot
cohort, artifact schema, review-status taxonomy, authority rules, or retrigger logic;
those are requirements decisions after the direction is confirmed.

## Confirmed Intent Handoff

**Upstream brief already fixes:** internal audience, monthly cadence, static HTML,
serverless deployment, metro/state coverage, screening rather than causal inference.

**Correct behavior:** State those values as fixed. Compare analytical directions such
as threshold-based monitoring, distributional change detection, or benchmark-relative
screening. Ask only about an unresolved choice that would alter that set. Do not ask
again who the audience is, whether cadence is monthly, or whether the output should be
interactive.

## Negative Boundary

**Input:** "The approved requirements specify a static Quarto dashboard, monthly data
refresh, metro/state filters, threshold flags, and a no-server constraint. Write the
implementation plan."

**Correct behavior:** Bypass idea refinement. The direction and requirements are
already selected; route directly to plan-first. Do not manufacture alternatives merely
because the skill is available.
