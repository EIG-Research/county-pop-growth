## Ideas Retained

- A three-phase divergent, convergent, and sharpening workflow.
- Selective use of reframing, inversion, simplification, constraint, analogy, and
  pre-mortem lenses.
- Materially distinct directions rather than a long list of shallow variations.
- Explicit central assumptions, failure conditions, evidence gaps, and exclusions.
- A concrete concept brief and user confirmation before downstream work.
- Repository inspection when refining an idea inside an existing codebase.
